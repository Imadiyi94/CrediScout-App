1. **Understand an application faster** — open any assessment hub: borrower, product,
   requested amount, stepper, and alerts in one screen.
2. **Structured assessment** — walk Stages 2→8 on the fixture; every stage persists
   + audits.
3. **Understand the numbers** — Stage 4 MetricCards and `/design`: every figure has
   meaning + interpretation.
4. **Identify important risks** — save weak figures at Stage 4/5; hub shows
   `WEAK_DSCR[HIGH]` etc. with clearing stages; fix figures → auto-resolve.
5. **Supportable exposure** — Stage 10 on the ₦5m fixture: assessed ₦2.4m with
   haircut rationale.
6. **Explicit recommendation** — Stage 11: REDUCED ₦2.4m × 12 mo, 5.00% RB,
   ₦270,781/mo schedule + EAR, reasons/risks/conditions.
7. **Correct automatic pricing** — `/admin/rates` dry-run: 3.2m Returning SBL → 5.00%;
   boundary probes (5m, 5,000,001, 10m, 50m) all resolve per §17.
8. **Explain the recommendation** — `/summary` + PDF: 18 items, decision-first,
   schedule attached.
